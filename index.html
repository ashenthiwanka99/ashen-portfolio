Ashen is an old word for the colour of ash. I took the hint: I like software that is light, fine-grained and quick to settle.

I am a senior full stack engineer with more than six years across B2B SaaS, travel tech and fintech. By day I build a multi-tenant platform at Docupath. Before that I shipped an AI restaurant booking platform, a trip-planning assistant and a PCI-compliant payments API. After hours I write small tools for my own desk: one sound on two headsets, software for my keyboard, shift lights for my wheel.
